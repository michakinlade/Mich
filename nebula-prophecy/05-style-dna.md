# STATE 5: Style DNA

*Baseline: one full transcript (19:28) plus three storylines. The numbers below come from one sample.*

## Niche
AI-generated cinematic fantasy short films in the survival, myth and creature genre. They play like a movie with very little talk: the pictures carry the story and the dialogue marks the turning points. Every video tells a new story in a new world, with the same kind of plot underneath.

## Target audience
A broad, mobile-first, global audience. Many viewers are not native English speakers, so the vocabulary stays simple. They're drawn to beauty-and-beast pairings, forbidden places, mythic creatures and underdog revenge. They watch for spectacle and feeling, not plot detail.

## Hook style
- **Cold-open line:** one out-of-context line of character voice over black before any setup. It sets a personality, not a plot.
- **Taboo warning within the first minute:** a second character names the forbidden place and says no one survives it. Danger is set up before the lead is.
- About 45 seconds of music-only footage before the first real line of dialogue.

## Script flow (6 chapters, about 3 minutes each, with titles on screen)
1. **Trespass:** the lead is pushed into the forbidden place by an outside threat, and a monster attack follows.
2. **Guardian confrontation:** "Who are you?", then an accusation. The lead insists they're innocent. The MacGuffin is named. The guardian offers a deal with a condition and does not explain it yet.
3. **Guardian backstory:** an object triggers a flashback. It shows the peaceful life the guardian had, then the empire arrives and takes everything. The villain gets a sneering monologue about claiming the land.
4. **Villain assault:** an attack plays out through battle commands. The guardian is captured and gets an ultimatum.
5. **Revenge reveal:** the captive turns out to be the supernatural force. The captors are destroyed, then comes a one-line vow of purpose.
6. **Pact and transformation:** the deal from Chapter 2 is finally explained as a rescue mission for a sibling. The lead gains powers and a creature to ride. The video ends mid-mission.

## Sentence rhythm
- Lines run 3–12 words. Longer lines are split into two clipped sentences.
- **Parallel openings, triads, either/or ultimatums** and **one-line proclamations**.
- A lot of silence between lines. Most lines stand alone as their own moment.

## Tone
Grave, mythic and fated. The guardian speaks in a lightly old-fashioned style. The lead speaks plainly and fearfully in modern English. The villains are arrogant and openly contemptuous.

## Transitions
- Chapter title cards named after what happens.
- Music bridges of 20–60 seconds between lines.
- A line of dialogue cuts straight into action, with no connecting narration.
- Flashbacks are triggered by an object or a question, and the backstory is told in first-person voiceover.

## Curiosity gaps
- The MacGuffin is named early with a mythic job and left unexplained.
- A conditional offer is planted in Chapter 2 and pays off in Chapter 6, with the same wording repeated.
- Vague references are clarified later ("one of mine" becomes "my sister").
- The guardian's true nature is kept back until the revenge scene.
- The ending leaves the mission unfinished.

## Emotional triggers
Dread, injustice, revenge payoff, awe, and protective urgency.

## Retention techniques
- A chapter structure that promises progress.
- Action spikes every 2–3 minutes.
- Withheld motives and repeated lines as callbacks.
- A beautiful woman or guardian set against a monstrous threat.
- The description ends with a "watch until the very end" call to action.

## Direct address
None inside the film. Direct address to the viewer happens only in the video description.

## Words per second
- Overall: about 0.51 WPS (roughly 600 spoken words in 1,168 seconds).
- While someone is speaking: about 2.0–2.4 WPS.
- About 55–60% of the runtime is music, screams or action with no speech.

## Word count
- Source: about 600 spoken words for a runtime of about 19.5 minutes.
- **Target: 600 words (±5% = 570–630), about 20 minutes, across 6 chapters of roughly 100 words each.**
