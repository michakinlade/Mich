# Nebula Prophecy — Drama Ideas (backlog)

1. **The Last Rider of the Ice Wastes.** A disgraced hunter is exiled into a frozen wasteland. He's saved from a blizzard by a giant white wolf, then learns that the tribe that banished him is poisoning the wolf's pack to take their hunting grounds.
2. **The Girl the Sea Returned.** A fisherman's daughter is lost in a storm and wakes up on a reef guarded by a colossal sea turtle. The mark on her back matches the one carved into a sunken temple.
3. **Beneath the Burning Dunes.** A caravan guard is stranded in the desert and hunted by a giant sand serpent. It turns out the serpent is fleeing the same slavers he was paid to protect.
4. **The Dragon Under the Mountain.** A young miner breaks into a sealed cavern and finds an old, dying dragon. His village's king has been draining its blood for gold, and only the miner's lineage can seal the wound.
5. **The Witch of the Black Marsh.** A soldier is sent to burn a swamp witch. He finds a healer who is sheltering the children his own army left orphaned.
6. **She Was Raised by Tigers.** An explorer is lost in a monsoon jungle and taken in by a tigress. Poachers are closing in on the valley, and one of them is someone she trusted.
7. **The Knight and the Forest Giant.** A knight on a quest to slay a giant finds out the "giant" is the last of a peaceful ancient race. His own lord wants it dead to claim the forest's timber.
8. **The Island That Breathes.** Shipwrecked sailors land on an island that turns out to be a sleeping sea titan. Their captain wants to harpoon it for its pearl heart.
9. **The Prince and the Phoenix.** An exiled prince climbs a volcano to capture a phoenix to win back his throne. He learns his uncle killed his father using the phoenix's stolen fire.
10. **The Shadow in the Snow Forest.** A Viking scout tracks a beast that attacks the village's hunters. It turns out to be a mother bear-spirit guarding a sacred spring the jarl plans to seize.
11. **The Boy Who Spoke to Wolves.** A mute shepherd boy is abandoned in the mountains and adopted by a wolf pack. Years later, he has to choose between the pack and the village hunting it.
12. **The Siren's Debt.** A cursed sea captain must bring a siren's stolen voice back from an emperor's vault, or his crew turns to stone at the next full moon.
13. **Lost City of the Serpent Queen.** A treasure hunter follows a map into a jungle ruin guarded by giant snakes. The ruin's last queen has been waiting for the bloodline the hunter doesn't know she belongs to.
14. **The Gladiator and the Beast.** An arena slave is ordered to kill a caged griffin before an emperor. The two of them plan an escape together instead.
15. **Where the Giants Sleep.** A lone warrior must cross a valley of sleeping stone giants to deliver a warning. One of them wakes, and it knows her name.
16. **The Monk and the Mountain Yeti.** A young monk is sent to find a missing pilgrim in the Himalayas. He finds that the yeti blamed for the disappearance saved the pilgrim from the bandits who robbed the temple.
17. **Queen of the Drowned Kingdom.** A diver searching for sunken gold finds an underwater city of merfolk. Its rulers believe she's the lost heir, and the treasure fleet above is coming to plunder it.
18. **The Hunter Who Became the Hunted.** A trophy hunter is dropped into a primeval jungle to hunt a living dinosaur. He ends up protecting its nest from the expedition that hired him.
19. **The Healer and the Wounded God.** A village healer secretly nurses a wounded forest god back to health, and the whole kingdom's army comes looking for it.
20. **The Warrior Without a Tribe.** An outcast archer is the only survivor of a massacre everyone blames on a jungle demon. She tracks the demon down and finds it survived the same massacre.
