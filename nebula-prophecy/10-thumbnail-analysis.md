# STATE 10: Thumbnail Analysis

*Based on 8 thumbnails. The creator-vlog one is left out because it doesn't use the storytelling format.*

## Text style
- **No text on any story thumbnail.** The image carries everything and the title does the talking.
- **Title formula:** a setup, an ellipsis, then a twist that gets cut off ("They Sent Him to Hunt It… But Seeing It C…", "They Left Him to Die in Chains… but the River C…").
- Common title words: "They," "Him," "Beautiful," "Finds," "Cursed," "Savage," "but."
- Titles are cut off on purpose, so the key twist is hidden when the title is shortened in the feed.

## Composition
- **Face-off between a human and a creature:** one person and one creature on opposite thirds, looking at each other, with an empty gap between them.
- **The human sits low and vulnerable:** sitting, kneeling or half-sunk in mud, water or sand, and often hurt, chained or holding a small blade.
- **The creature is higher or approaching:** full-body and clearly readable, never hidden.
- Wide or medium-wide framing with both full figures in shot and the setting readable.
- Group variant: a mass of creatures or soldiers around one central figure.

## Color contrast
- **Brighter and cleaner than the video frames.** Daylight, HDR-crisp and sharp everywhere, with almost no background blur.
- **Muted natural palette:** mud brown, moss green, slate blue sea, overcast grey sky.
- **Warm skin set against cool or grey backgrounds**, with small bright accents: gold armor, red eyes, a pink tongue, spotted fur.
- Wet, glistening textures and detailed skies with storm clouds or mist.

## Emotion triggers
- **Vulnerability against power.**
- **"Will it attack or help?"**
- **Beauty and the beast.**
- **Awe at scale.**
- **Primal danger:** blades drawn, chains, bared teeth.
- **Twist implied by the title.**
