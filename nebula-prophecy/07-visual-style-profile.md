# STATE 7: Visual Style Profile

*Built from 4 unique frames: masked jungle warrior, sci-fi lab, hangar boarding ramp, astronaut in a cave.*

## Art style
- Photorealistic cinematic live-action look, like a still from a Hollywood feature. No illustration and no stylization.
- Real-looking skin, sweat, grime and fabric. Faces look like real actors.
- A blockbuster production feel: practical-looking sets, wardrobe and props.

## Color palette
- Desaturated, with a cool cast overall and heavy teal-and-green grading.
- Jungle scenes: deep moss green, muted olive and wet brown, with small warm accents in skin and blood.
- Sci-fi scenes: steel blue, navy black and cold cyan LED highlights.
- Skin tones stay warm against the cool backgrounds. Blacks are crushed but keep some detail.

## Lighting style
- Low-key and moody, with about 60–70% of the frame in shadow.
- Motivated light sources: diffuse overcast light through the canopy, practical LEDs, monitor glow, or a single hard source in caves.
- Soft rim light separating the subject from the background.
- Atmospheric haze or mist adds depth. No flat or bright fill light.

## Camera style
- Anamorphic widescreen at 2.39:1 with letterbox bars.
- Shallow depth of field with creamy background blur.
- **Lenses:** 35–85mm equivalent for medium shots and close-ups, wide lenses for scale.
- Eye-level or slightly low angles. Sometimes shot through foreground foliage or objects, as if the camera is watching from hiding.
- Subtle film grain and gentle softness, like a digital cinema camera.

## Composition
- Subject placed on a rule-of-thirds line or dead center in close-ups of fear or rage.
- Foreground layers such as leaves, rock edges or equipment create depth.
- Side-profile group shots moving across the frame for procession scenes.
- Faces take up 30–50% of the frame in emotional beats. Plenty of negative space goes into dark backgrounds.

## Detail level
- High micro-detail: dirt specks, sweat beads, scratches, wet hair strands, worn and scuffed wardrobe, weathered metal.
- Environments are dense but softly blurred behind the subject.

## Mood
- Tense, gritty, survival-driven and ominous.
- Emotions are raw and physical: fear, rage, exhaustion.
- It feels grounded and serious, like a premium streaming film, not fantasy art.

## Style tag used in every prompt
> Photorealistic cinematic film still, anamorphic 2.39:1 widescreen, shallow depth of field, desaturated teal-green color grade, low-key moody lighting with soft rim light, atmospheric haze, subtle film grain, high micro-detail on skin, dirt and textures, gritty survival-thriller mood, Hollywood blockbuster production quality.
