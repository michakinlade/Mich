## Channel description: final (recommended)
The money stories nobody tells you.

Somber Journal is animated storytelling about how money quietly changes your life, and the people in it.

Every story is told to you, about you. What your family stops telling you at every level of wealth. What leaves first when you lose everything. Why two friends with the same paycheck end up living two completely different lives. How banks, landlords and the people you love see you differently at −$3,000 and at $10 million.

No get-rich-quick. No gurus. No financial advice. Just honest stories about money, family, pride, and what it costs to climb or to fall.

New stories every week. Subscribe if you've ever felt the number in your account change the way people look at you.

All stories are fiction. Characters are composites and numbers are illustrative.

## Banner prompts (v2, built on your existing character and wordmark)

### Layout spec for adding text in Canva or Photoshop
- **Canvas:** 2560 x 1440 px. Keep all text and the character inside the central 1546 x 423 safe zone (x 507–2053, y 508–931).
- **Wordmark:** keep your current one. "somber" in Montserrat ExtraBold (or Poppins Bold); "journal" in Montserrat Light / Thin. White (#F2EEE6). About 150–170 px tall.
- **Gold line:** 2 px, muted gold (#C9A45C), the same width as the wordmark, 20 px below it.
- **Tagline:** "The money stories nobody tells you." in Montserrat Regular, 44–48 px, letter-spacing +8%, off-white.
- **Small line:** "New stories every week" in Montserrat Regular, 30 px, 60% opacity.
- **Remove:** the Subscribe button.
- **Check:** preview with YouTube's banner tool on mobile, desktop and TV before saving.

## Switching checklist
1. Check that @SomberJournal is free on YouTube.
2. YouTube Studio → Customization → Basic info: change the name, the handle and the description.
3. Upload the new logo, banner and watermark.
4. Add the channel keywords.
5. Update the pinned comment and descriptions on videos 1 and 2 with the new tagline.
6. Publish video 3 under the new name. The handle change keeps the same channel, subscribers and videos.
