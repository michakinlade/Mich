## PART 1: SCRIPT
====================================================

### Voice
- Second person, present tense, 100% of the time. The viewer is the protagonist.
- The protagonist is never named. Supporting characters get simple first names.
- The narrator is calm, intimate and slightly clinical. Warm toward people, cold toward systems.
- Name the missing or unexpected feeling, not the obvious one ("Not anger. Just tired.").
- No intro, no channel name, no "subscribe", no advice, no headings, no emojis, no visual notes.

### Rhythm
- Mostly short declaratives of 4–10 words, plus fragments.
- One long cascading sentence of 25–40 words every 5–7 lines.
- Use negation runs ("Not X. Not Y. Just Z.") and repeat lines for weight.
- End most paragraphs on a 3–6 word punch.
- Numbers are always exact and slightly odd ($4, $35, $3,200, 6.8%, 7:12 on a Friday).

### Hook (first 150–250 words)
- Open mid-scene with a specific time, an exact amount and one physical object.
- Set a mundane action against a hidden fact or an unfair system.
- Land the first twist by word 60–120.
- Deliver the thesis line by word 150–250 as forbidden knowledge.

### Format A: Level Ladder (7–9 levels)
- Each level starts "Level [N], $[amount]."
- Each level follows this loop:
  1. A state change
  2. Two or three concrete micro-scenes
  3. A callback to a lower level ("The same ___ that ___ at $200 now ___ at $2,000.")
  4. A realization
  5. A verdict: "Rule [N]. At $[amount], you're not ___. You're ___."
- Low levels get painful, sensory detail. High levels get access, abstraction and influence.
- Plant 3–4 objects or numbers early and echo them later as memories.
- Outro: "Tonight, somewhere…" replays Level 1 with a stranger, then one closing thesis line.

### Format B: Life Drama (one life, 10–15 years)
- Cold open with a secret.
- Origin with exact age, salary, savings, debt and net worth.
- An origin ritual that pays off at the end.
- One small secret rule.
- Acts that compress the years, each with 2–3 scenes and a foreshadowing closer.
- A false resolution right before the collapse.
- A dark turn in which the money or the secret starts to corrupt a relationship.
- The climax returns to the cold open and the secret is revealed.
- The ending echoes the origin with a quiet triad or a shared silence.

### Retention
- A quotable aphorism every 150–230 words.
- Every section ends on an open loop.
- Escalating numbers and 2–3 recurring motifs that pay off.
- At least one tender moment and one morally uncomfortable moment.

### Originality
- Every sentence is newly written, with fresh names, numbers and settings.

### Part 1 output
1. "TARGET: [N] words"
2. The script as plain paragraphs
3. "FINAL: [N] words · est. runtime [mm:ss]"
